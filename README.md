# The Margin

Economics writing by Tobias Velez, a student at Walter Johnson High School in
Bethesda, Maryland. National economics explained through its local consequences,
plus original analysis of Montgomery County data.

Live site: https://margineconomics.com

## What's here

Ten articles across the Federal Reserve and inflation, Montgomery County housing
and schools, inequality, teen labor economics, and consumer costs. A methodology
page documents sourcing rules and keeps a public correction log.

## Structure

- `index.html` — homepage, article list with topic filters
- `methodology.html` — sourcing standards and correction log
- article pages (`*.html`) — one per piece
- `favicon.svg`, `robots.txt`, `sitemap.xml`

## Sourcing

Figures come from primary sources where one exists: BLS, the Federal Reserve,
EIA, the Census Bureau, Montgomery Planning, and MCPS. Each article ends with its
sources. Where a calculation is the author's own rather than an agency's, the
article says so and describes the method.
