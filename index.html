About

The Margin is written by Tobias Velez, a student at Walter Johnson High School in Bethesda, Maryland.

Figures come from primary sources where one exists: the Bureau of Labor Statistics, the Federal Reserve, the Energy Information Administration, the Census Bureau, Montgomery Planning and MCPS. Each article ends with its sources. Where a calculation is mine rather than an agency’s, the article says so and describes the method.
